2. What we collect
  • Account data: name, username, email, and a securely hashed password (we never store your actual password).
  • Content you create: tasks, notes, goals, planner entries, attendance, timetable, and similar study data you enter into the app.

3. How we use it

To provide the app's features, respond to AI queries, send password-reset emails, and improve the service. We do not sell your data.

4. Third parties we share data with
  • Groq — processes text/image inputs to AI features to generate responses.
  • Pollinations.ai — generates illustrative images for the AI Visual Explainer feature; the concept you type is sent to their service to create the image.
5. Data retention & deletion

Your data is retained while your account is active. You can permanently delete your account and all associated data yourself, at any time, from Settings → Danger Zone → Delete My Account — no need to email us. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

6. Security

Passwords are stored using a salted one-way hash (scrypt) — we never store or have access to your actual password. Session cookies are HttpOnly and restricted to same-site requests. File uploads used by AI features (images/PDFs) are deleted from our servers immediately after processing.

7. Children's privacy

This app is intended for students and may be used by minors under adult/guardian awareness. We do not knowingly collect more personal information than needed to provide the study-planner and AI-tutoring features described above, and we do not use student data for advertising or sell it to third parties.

8. Data safety summary
  • Collected: account info, app-usage content you create, and AI feature inputs (see Section 2).

  • You can request deletion: yes, in-app and immediately (Section 5).
